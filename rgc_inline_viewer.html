July 2021 Failure Mechanism

Anti-surge valve stuck open at 66.6%. 2/3 of gas recycled back to suction. Motor drew full power (144.4 A) but net downstream flow crashed by -8.0σ. Acoustic recycle turbulence spiked vibration to 7.3 mm/s (Danger Zone D).

Timeline Analysis: Motor current (top), discharge pressure (middle), and anti-surge valve (bottom) illustrating unpowered standby (Jan 2020), baseline loaded operation (March 2020), and the persistent recycle crisis in mid-2021.

Aerodynamic Recirculation vs Bearing Seizure Diagnosis

When casing vibration spiked to 7.3 mm/s (ISO Zone D Danger), a standard threshold alarm might lead maintenance to mistakenly order an expensive mechanical bearing overhaul.

Bearing Temperatures: Normal

NDE bearing temps remained stable at 54-57°C. Lubrication oil headers and journal friction were healthy.

Anti-Surge Bypass: 66.6% Open

High-velocity bypass back into suction caused aerodynamic turbulence, pressure fluctuations, and acoustic casing shudder.

✅ Prescription: Operators must inspect and unstick the pneumatic actuator on anti-surge valve CCRNHIC0773A.PV, instead of tearing down the compressor bearings.
