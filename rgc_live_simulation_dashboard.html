📐 Mathematical Architecture of the Digital Twin

Industrial compressor telemetry cannot be simulated with naive independent Gaussian noise because adjacent 5-minute samples possess high temporal autocorrelation (ρ ≈ 0.95) and coupled cross-subsystem physics (e.g. flow correlates with power and suction pressure).

1. Multivariate AR(1) Dynamics

The state vector Xt transitions according to:

Xt = μ + Φ(Xt−1 − μ) + εt

where Φ = diag(φ&sub1;, ..., φ&sub1;&sub5;) captures individual sensor momentum calibrated to real baseline empirical autocorrelation (0.85 ≤ φ ≤ 0.98).

2. Cholesky Cross-Covariance

Driving noise εt preserves physical sensor coupling:

εt = L · wt,   wt ∼ N(0, I)

where L is the lower Cholesky decomposition of regularized noise covariance: Σε = L · LT.

3. Diurnal Waves & Physics Bounds

Adds 24-hour diurnal ambient waves on lube oil and suction temps:

Dt = A · sin(2πt / 288)

Enforces hard operational physical limits: Q ≥ 0 m³/h, I ≥ 135 A, 0% ≤ ASV ≤ 100%.
